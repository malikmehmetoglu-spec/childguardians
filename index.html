Every child deserves dignity, not just aid

We work inside the communities of Durban — Chatsworth, Welbedacht, Emona, Tongaat and Fairbreeze — with children who are too often counted and rarely asked. Food, clothing, learning: delivered so that children choose, take part, and feel seen.

Who we are

We live the work, not visit it

During Ramadan our team did not drop in for photographs. We stayed inside Darul Uloom for the month, sharing the children's routine, hearing what they actually needed, and letting that shape every decision. Children are never passive recipients of our programmes; their views change how we deliver.

  • Children choose

    46 boys picked their own Eid clothing in-store, within an agreed budget.

  • Volunteers deliver

    Every stage — from livestock selection to the last home delivery — run by our own team.
